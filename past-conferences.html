Conference Highlights Through the Years

From our humble beginnings at a St. Petersburg bar to international recognition, here are some memorable moments from our conferences.

Diverse Presentations

From traditional papers to short performances, debates, and Author Meets Critics sessions, our conferences feature a variety of formats that bring humor philosophy to life.

Global Participation

Scholars from across the nation and around the world have joined us to share their work on humor and philosophy, from California to Lithuania.

Lasting Friendships

Many attendees return year after year, creating a tight-knit community of scholars who share a love of philosophy and laughter.
