Conference Travel Tips

Planning Your Trip

  • Book accommodations early, especially for venues in popular areas
  • Check if the conference hotel offers special rates for attendees
  • Consider arriving a day early to adjust and explore the area
  • Keep conference organizer contact information handy

At the Conference

  • Registration typically opens the morning of the first day
  • Bring business cards to exchange with fellow scholars
  • Don't miss the social events—they're often the highlight!
  • Consider volunteering as a heckler (commentator) for sessions

Questions?

For specific questions about venue accessibility, nearby accommodations, or travel arrangements, please contact the conference organizers.
